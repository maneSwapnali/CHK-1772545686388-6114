Digital Safety Learning Center

Essential knowledge to protect yourself from modern cyber threats and online fraud.

01
🔐

Use Strong, Unique Passwords

Never reuse passwords across sites. Use a password manager to generate 16+ character passwords with mixed characters. Enable two-factor authentication on every account.

Critical
02
🕵️

Verify Sender Identity

Hover over links before clicking. Check the actual email address, not just the display name. Legitimate organizations never ask for passwords via email.

Critical
03
⏰

Beware of Urgency Tactics

Scammers create false urgency: "Act now!", "Your account will be closed in 24 hours!" Legitimate companies give you time. When in doubt, contact the company directly.

Critical
04
🔒

Always Check for HTTPS

Before entering any personal information, ensure the site uses HTTPS (padlock icon). However, HTTPS alone doesn't guarantee a site is safe — phishers use it too.

Important
05
💳

Protect Financial Information

Never share card numbers, PINs, CVVs, or banking credentials over phone or email. Banks never ask for your full password. Use virtual cards for online shopping.

Critical
06
🌐

Spot Fake Websites

Look for subtle misspellings: paypa1.com, amaz0n.net, g00gle.com. Check the full domain name carefully. Use URL scanners before entering sensitive data.

Important
07
📱

Beware of SMS Phishing (Smishing)

Fake delivery notifications, bank alerts, and prize messages via SMS are common. Never click links in unexpected text messages. Navigate to sites directly.

Important
08
🔄

Keep Software Updated

Updates patch security vulnerabilities. Enable automatic updates for your OS, browser, and apps. Outdated software is a primary entry point for malware attacks.

Essential
09
🎣

Recognize Spear Phishing

Targeted attacks use your name, company, or recent activity to appear legitimate. Be skeptical of any unsolicited communication, even if it contains accurate details about you.

Critical
10
👥

Protect Your Social Media

Oversharing on social media helps criminals craft convincing scams. Review privacy settings, limit personal information publicly visible, and be cautious about friend requests from strangers.

Essential
11
📊

Monitor Your Accounts

Regularly review bank statements and credit reports. Set up transaction alerts. Early detection of unauthorized activity minimizes damage from identity theft.

Essential
12
🛡️

Report What You Find

Reporting scams helps protect others. Use our Report page to submit threats, or contact national cybercrime reporting agencies in your country. Every report matters.

Essential
